# Starter Checklist

Print this page, or check boxes right in the built site. Everything here is
doable in under a week of evenings, of which the whole setup is about three
hours total.

## Before you list (week 0, about one evening)

- [ ] Choose **two categories** from [What sells](01-what-sells.md). Write them down.
- [ ] Make (or claim) your **seller account** on one marketplace; fill the
      every-field version — real name, bank/deposit details handled there,
      never anywhere else.
- [ ] Turn on **two-factor sign-in** on email *and* marketplace accounts
      (a code on every login beats [scam chapters](07-scam-proofing.md)
      admirably).
- [ ] Make your **shipping station**: a corner with tape, three box sizes,
      two envelope sizes — see [shipping without stress](04-shipping-without-stress.md).
- [ ] Buy or borrow the **$12 scale**. This is the one purchase this page
      endorses.

## First listing day (evening two)

- [ ] Photograph one item outdoors/indoor-window with the plain background
      and the [8-photo shot list](03-photos-and-listings.md).
- [ ] Look up **sold prices** for it; write your researched price and your
      walk-away floor on paper.
- [ ] Run the [four-question test](01-what-sells.md) once more; then list.
- [ ] Fill item specifics honestly (brand, model, size, condition).
- [ ] Add it as row #1 in the [records table](06-keeping-records.md).

## Before your first sale ships (ten minutes, any day)

- [ ] Read [Scam-proofing](07-scam-proofing.md) once, for the index-card
      four-question test. Reply to everything *in the platform*.
- [ ] Know your **drop-off points**: post office plus one alternative.
- [ ] Print nothing yet — labels come in-app — but find where they'd go
      (your marketplace's "Orders" page).

## Week one numbers (the whole of financial setup)

- [ ] Decide your **sourcing budget per trip** ($20 round is plenty) and
      pay cash where possible.
- [ ] Start the **TAXES envelope**; first transfer due after your first
      profitable sale — roughly a quarter of profit per the
      [fees chapter](05-fees-and-taxes.md).
- [ ] Bookmark your marketplace **sold listings** search — the pricing
      ritual you'll use every sourcing and reprice day.

## Habit-planting (the rest of month one)

- [ ] One **sourcing trip** per week, 40 minutes, two categories, walk
      away three times on purpose.
- [ ] One **batch** of listings per week: shoot 5–10 at once, draft, then
      publish.
- [ ] One **drop-off day** per week; pack photo taken before the tape
      seals.
- [ ] One **monthly twenty minutes**: totals row, envelope, photo
      receipts, back up the file ([keeping records](06-keeping-records.md)).
- [ ] Reprice anything sitting past **day 14** per the markdown clock.
- [ ] After week two, read [Quitting politely](08-quitting-politely.md)
      once — not because you'll quit; because you'll know the door is
      unlocked. It makes staying *nicer*.

## The line at the bottom of every checklist ever written

If a checkbox here doesn't fit your life, cross it out and carry on.
The system exists to serve the side hustle, which serves the life —
in that order, always.
